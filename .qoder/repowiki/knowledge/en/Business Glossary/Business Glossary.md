### StockWise POS
- Definition：The product name of this application — a point-of-sale and inventory system for single or multi-store retail businesses, supporting both direct sales and an invoice-approval workflow.
- Aliases：StockWise

### layby
- Definition：A lay-by sale mode where a customer reserves items and pays over time before collection; supported alongside direct sales and the invoice-approval flow.
- Aliases：layby sale

### direct sale
- Definition：POS transaction type where the cashier completes the sale immediately without requiring a second user's approval.

### approved based system
- Definition：POS transaction type where a cashier creates an invoice that must be approved by another user before it becomes effective.
- Aliases：invoice approval flow

### PIN-based role
- Definition：Authentication model where a shared email/password login is followed by a four-digit PIN that encodes the user's role (cashier vs admin).
- Aliases：four-digit PIN
